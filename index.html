Undo for the stuff
you take apart.

Snap a photo after each part you remove. When it's time to rebuild, UndoTable circles where every piece came from and walks you back in reverse.

This page needs JavaScript: the photo comparer and the image models run inside your browser, and nothing is uploaded. The first comparison downloads about 110 MB of models once.

  1. Snap the whole thing from above.
  2. Remove a part, move it out of the frame, snap again. Repeat.
  3. Put it back: steps in reverse, every part circled on your own photo.
  4. Mystery screw? Photograph it. The app finds where it came from.

How it works

OpenCV.js lines two photos up with ORB keypoints and a RANSAC homography, then finds what disappeared with a Lab colour difference. A DINOv2-small vector stores each part, and a CLIP vector suggests a name from 50 part names. Teardowns are kept in IndexedDB on your device.

Tested on four sets of AI-generated photos: 20 of 20 removed parts were detected with no false alarms.
